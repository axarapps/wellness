Loving Light Visualization

Light Body Meditation

Visualization

How to

Imagine warm light entering with each inhale and spreading through the body, dissolving tension wherever it settles, then imagine that same light radiating outward beyond the body with each exhale.

Benefits

  • A gentle, modern visualization with no specific tradition required
  • Combines well with progressive relaxation for a calming close to a session
  • Easy to adapt to any length of time available

Practice

10-20 minutes, lying down or seated, following the light at the pace of natural breathing.
