Mantra Chanting

Kirtan

Sound

How to

Chant a traditional mantra or sacred phrase aloud, alone or with others, letting the sound and vibration of the voice itself become the focus rather than the words' literal meaning.

Benefits

  • The vocalization itself has a physically calming, vagal effect
  • A more energizing, active alternative to silent practices
  • Traditionally practiced in groups, which adds a communal dimension

Practice

10-20 minutes, chanting at a comfortable volume and pace rather than forcing volume or duration.
