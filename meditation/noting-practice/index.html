Noting Practice

Vipassana Noting

Self inquiry

How to

As each thought, sound, or sensation arises, silently label it with a single soft word — "thinking," "hearing," "feeling" — then let it go and return to a resting attention, without engaging the content of what arose.

Benefits

  • Creates useful distance from the content of thoughts and reactions
  • Makes the habitual, automatic quality of mental activity visible
  • A precise technique often used in intensive Vipassana retreats

Practice

15-20 minutes; keep labels brief and soft rather than turning them into a running commentary.
