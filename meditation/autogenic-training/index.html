Autogenic Training

Self-Relaxation Training

Deep relaxation

How to

Lying or sitting comfortably, silently repeat a set sequence of phrases describing physical states — "my right arm is heavy," "my hands are warm" — allowing the body to genuinely settle into each sensation rather than forcing it.

Benefits

  • A structured, verbal alternative to open-ended relaxation
  • Developed by psychiatrist Johannes Schultz in the early twentieth century
  • Effects tend to strengthen with regular, repeated practice

Practice

10-15 minutes, moving through the phrase sequence for each body area only once or twice per session.
