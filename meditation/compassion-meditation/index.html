Compassion Meditation

Karuna Bhavana

Loving kindness

How to

Bring to mind a being who is suffering, and let a genuine wish for their relief arise — "may you be free from this pain" — without needing to fix or resolve their situation, then extend the same wish to yourself.

Benefits

  • Distinct from Metta's warmth by focusing specifically on relief from suffering
  • Reduces compassion fatigue in caregiving or helping roles
  • Extending it to oneself addresses self-compassion directly

Practice

10-15 minutes; if a specific person's suffering feels too raw to hold, start with a more distant example before returning to it.
