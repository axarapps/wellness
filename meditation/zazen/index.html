Zazen

Shikantaza

Self inquiry

How to

Sit upright facing a blank wall, eyes half-open and unfocused, doing "just sitting" — not following the breath, a mantra, or any other object, simply remaining fully present and alert without an object of concentration at all.

Benefits

  • The core seated practice of Soto Zen Buddhism
  • Trains presence without relying on any anchor
  • Often practiced in fixed time blocks marked by a bell

Practice

20-30 minutes seated in a stable posture; a timer or bell removes the need to track time during the sit.
