Chakra Visualization

Chakra Dhyana

Visualization

How to

Moving attention from the base of the spine to the crown of the head, visualize each of the seven energy centers in turn as a glowing wheel of its traditional color, pausing at each before moving upward.

Benefits

  • Gives a structured, full-body focus for visualization practice
  • A traditional tantric framework still widely used in modern meditation
  • Works well as a longer, more absorbing session

Practice

15-25 minutes, spending roughly equal time at each center rather than lingering only on familiar ones.
