Sound Bath

Singing Bowl Meditation

Sound

How to

Sit or lie comfortably while a singing bowl, gong, or other resonant instrument is played (live or recorded), letting attention follow the sound as it rises, sustains, and fades rather than the silence between notes.

Benefits

  • An easy entry point for people new to meditation
  • The sustained tones naturally slow and settle attention
  • Works well as a guided, externally-paced session

Practice

15-30 minutes; a recording works as well as a live instrument for home practice.
