Self-Inquiry

Atma Vichara

Self inquiry

How to

Whenever a thought, feeling, or identity arises, ask directly "who is experiencing this?" or "who am I?", not to produce a conceptual answer but to trace attention back to the sense of self that is aware in the first place.

Benefits

  • A direct, non-dual approach rather than a concentration or relaxation technique
  • Popularized in the modern era by the sage Ramana Maharshi
  • Best suited to those with some existing meditation experience

Practice

15-20 minutes, returning to the question gently each time it is forgotten rather than analyzing it intellectually.
