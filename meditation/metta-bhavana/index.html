Metta Bhavana

Loving-Kindness Meditation

Loving kindness

How to

Sitting quietly, silently offer a set phrase — such as "may you be safe, may you be at ease" — first to yourself, then in turn to someone you love, a neutral person, someone you find difficult, and finally all beings.

Benefits

  • Builds warmth and connection toward others and oneself
  • Softens self-criticism over repeated practice
  • Shown to increase positive emotion even over short daily sessions

Practice

10-20 minutes, spending a few unhurried minutes on each person in turn rather than rushing to cover everyone.
