Cheek Lifter

Smile lift, no teeth

Cheeks

How to

Smile without showing your teeth, lifting the corners of your mouth up toward your ears and feeling the cheeks rise. Place two fingers lightly on the cheeks to feel the muscle engage.

Benefits

  • Trains the muscles that lift the cheek, not just the mouth
  • A close relative of a genuine smile, so it feels natural rather than forced
  • Easy to layer into an existing smiling habit

Practice

Hold 10 seconds, release, repeat 8-10 times.
