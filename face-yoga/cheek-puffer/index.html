Cheek Puffer

Cheek muscle isolation

Cheeks

How to

Take a breath and puff both cheeks out with air, holding it while keeping the lips sealed. Move the air from one cheek to the other a few times before releasing.

Benefits

  • Works the cheek muscles directly, something few everyday expressions do
  • The side-to-side air shift adds a mobility element beyond a simple hold
  • A recognizable, easy-to-remember move

Practice

Hold the puff 10-15 seconds, shifting air side to side, then release fully. Repeat 3-4 times.
