Vowel Sequence

Exaggerated A-E-I-O-U

Full face dynamic

How to

Mouth each vowel sound — A, E, I, O, U — one at a time, exaggerating the mouth shape far more than you would in normal speech, and holding each shape briefly before moving to the next.

Benefits

  • Moves the mouth and cheeks through a wide variety of shapes in one exercise
  • Doubles as a light warm-up before speaking or presenting
  • Easy to do anywhere since it borrows shapes from ordinary speech

Practice

Hold each vowel shape 2-3 seconds. One full A-E-I-O-U cycle, repeated 5-6 times.
