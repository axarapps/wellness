Cheekbone Sculptor

Cheekbone definition

Cheeks

How to

Press two fingers gently onto the tops of your cheekbones. Smile upward against the light resistance of your fingers, feeling the lift concentrated right under the cheekbone.

Benefits

  • Focuses the lift higher on the face than a full smile does
  • A common technique for emphasizing cheekbone contour
  • Combines well with Cheek Lifter as a two-part cheek routine

Practice

Hold 8-10 seconds, release, repeat 8-10 times.
