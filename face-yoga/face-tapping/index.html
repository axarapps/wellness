Face Tapping

Circulation, light tapping

Full face dynamic

How to

Using your fingertips, tap lightly and quickly across your forehead, cheeks, jaw, and neck, moving in small sections rather than pressing anywhere firmly.

Benefits

  • A gentle way to bring blood flow to the surface of the face
  • Feels alerting without any of the effort of a held exercise
  • A pleasant way to close out a face-yoga session

Practice

20-30 seconds total, moving continuously across the whole face and neck.
