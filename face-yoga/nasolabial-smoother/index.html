Nasolabial Smoother

Mouth O, lips over teeth

Lips mouth

How to

Shape your mouth into a soft "O," then roll your lips inward slightly over your teeth while keeping the O shape. Hold gently, feeling the area beside the nose and mouth engage.

Benefits

  • Targets the folds that run from the nose to the corners of the mouth
  • A gentle, low-effort version of a common face-yoga mouth shape
  • Easy to check in a mirror the first few times to confirm the shape

Practice

Hold 10 seconds, release, repeat 8-10 times.
