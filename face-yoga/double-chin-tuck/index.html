Double-Chin Tuck

Chin-to-chest tuck

Jaw chin

How to

Sitting tall, tuck your chin gently toward your chest without rounding your upper back, pressing your tongue to the roof of your mouth as you do. Hold, then release back to neutral.

Benefits

  • Targets the muscles under the chin often associated with a softer jawline
  • The tongue press adds engagement without needing to strain the neck
  • A good addition to a desk-break routine

Practice

Hold 5-8 seconds, release to neutral, repeat 10-12 times.
