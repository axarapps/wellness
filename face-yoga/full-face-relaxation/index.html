Full-Face Relaxation

Whole-face wind-down

Relaxation cooldown

How to

Sit or lie comfortably, close your eyes, and let your whole face go soft and heavy — forehead, brow, cheeks, jaw, and lips all resting with no effort at all.

Benefits

  • A complete, passive close to any face-yoga session
  • Reinforces the difference between working a muscle and truly resting it
  • Calming enough to use on its own at the end of a long day

Practice

Hold 45-60 seconds, breathing slowly and letting each exhale soften the face further.
