Neck Tilt Press

Side neck, resisted

Neck under chin

How to

Tilt your head toward one shoulder, then press your hand lightly against the side of your head while trying to tilt back toward center, holding the position without actually moving.

Benefits

  • Adds isometric resistance to a simple neck tilt
  • Engages the neck muscles rather than only stretching them
  • A useful counterpart to a passive neck stretch

Practice

Hold 8-10 seconds per side, breathing normally throughout.
