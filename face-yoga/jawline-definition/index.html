Jawline Definition

Jaw jut, forward

Jaw chin

How to

Jut your lower jaw forward slightly, past your upper teeth, and hold. You should feel the muscles along the jawline and upper neck engage.

Benefits

  • Targets the muscles along the jawline directly
  • A common technique aimed at a more defined jaw contour over time
  • Simple enough to do while seated anywhere

Practice

Hold 10 seconds, release, repeat 8-10 times.
