The Owl

Eyes up, brow still

Eyes

How to

Keeping your head level and your eyebrows relaxed, look upward with just your eyes as far as comfortable, hold, then look straight ahead again. Repeat looking down, then to each side.

Benefits

  • Exercises the muscles around the eye without involving the forehead at all
  • A gentle reminder of how much the eyes can move on their own
  • Doubles as a short break from a fixed screen-distance stare

Practice

Hold each direction 5 seconds. One full up-down-left-right cycle, repeated 3-4 times.
