Wide-Eye Lift

Upper-lid firming

Eyes

How to

Open your eyes as wide as comfortable without raising your eyebrows — use a finger resting lightly on the brow to keep it still. Hold the wide-eyed position, then release.

Benefits

  • Firms the upper eyelid independently of the forehead
  • Useful for anyone whose eyes look tired even when well rested
  • Isolates a muscle that's easy to neglect since the brow usually does the work instead

Practice

Hold 5-8 seconds, then close the eyes gently to rest. Repeat 8-10 times.
