Full-Face Warm-Up

Scrunch and release

Full face dynamic

How to

Scrunch your entire face tightly toward the center — eyes shut, nose wrinkled, lips pursed — hold briefly, then release everything at once and let the face go completely slack.

Benefits

  • A quick way to warm up every part of the face before a longer routine
  • The contrast between full tension and full release is easy to feel
  • A natural first move to start any face-yoga session

Practice

Scrunch 3-5 seconds, release fully for 5 seconds, repeat 4-5 times.
