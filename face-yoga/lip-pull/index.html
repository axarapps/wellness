Lip Pull

Wide smile, then pucker

Lips mouth

How to

Stretch your lips into a wide, closed-mouth smile as if saying a long "eee," hold, then purse them into a tight "ooo" shape. Alternate between the two shapes smoothly.

Benefits

  • Moves the muscles around the mouth through their full range in one exercise
  • A common warm-up move before other lip and cheek exercises
  • Easy to remember since it mirrors two everyday sounds

Practice

Hold each shape 5 seconds, alternating for 8-10 total repetitions.
