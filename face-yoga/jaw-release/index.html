Jaw Release

Jaw & TMJ tension release

Jaw chin

How to

Let your jaw drop open naturally, without forcing it wide, and let it hang loose for a moment. Gently move it side to side and slightly forward before letting it close softly.

Benefits

  • Releases tension that builds up from clenching or grinding through the day
  • A gentle way to check in on jaw tightness before it becomes discomfort
  • Often felt immediately as a release around the ears and temples

Practice

Hold the open, relaxed position 15-20 seconds, breathing normally throughout.
