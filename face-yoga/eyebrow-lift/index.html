Eyebrow Lift

Brow lift, resisted

Forehead brow

How to

Place two fingers just above each eyebrow. Gently raise both eyebrows against the resistance of your fingers, hold, then lower slowly without letting the forehead crease.

Benefits

  • Strengthens the muscles that lift the brow instead of just stretching them
  • A useful counter to a habitual furrowed or lowered brow
  • Simple enough to fit between other tasks

Practice

Hold 10-15 seconds, then lower slowly over a count of 3. Repeat 5-8 times.
