Smile Resistance

Smile against fingers

Lips mouth

How to

Place a finger lightly at each corner of your mouth. Try to smile against the gentle resistance of your fingers, feeling the effort concentrated at the corners of the mouth.

Benefits

  • Adds light resistance training to a movement usually done with no effort at all
  • Focuses specifically on the corners of the mouth
  • Pairs naturally with Cheek Lifter for a fuller lower-face routine

Practice

Hold 8-10 seconds, release, repeat 8-10 times.
