Eye Press & Release

Circulation, closed-eye

Eyes

How to

Close your eyes gently and squeeze the lids shut for a few seconds, then release completely and let them rest closed and soft before opening.

Benefits

  • Encourages circulation around the eye through simple contract-and-release
  • A calming pause if the eyes feel dry or strained
  • Can be done anywhere without anyone noticing

Practice

Squeeze 3-5 seconds, rest 5 seconds closed, repeat 6-8 times.
