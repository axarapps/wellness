Seated Figure-4 Stretch

Hip & glute release

Hips glutes

How to

Sitting tall in a chair, cross one ankle over the opposite knee. Keeping the crossed foot flexed, gently hinge forward at the hips or press the raised knee down until a stretch is felt through the outer hip.

Benefits

  • Releases tight hips and glutes from prolonged sitting
  • Can ease lower-back tension that stems from hip tightness
  • Needs nothing but a chair — works at a desk

Practice

Hold 20-30 seconds per side, keeping the crossed ankle flexed rather than pointed to protect the knee.
