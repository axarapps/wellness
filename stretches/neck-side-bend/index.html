Neck Side Bend

Neck & upper trapezius release

Neck and shoulders

How to

Sitting or standing tall, tilt your ear toward one shoulder until a gentle stretch is felt along the opposite side of the neck. Rest your hand lightly on top of your head for a little extra weight — don't pull.

Benefits

  • Releases tension built up from screen time and desk posture
  • Eases tightness that contributes to tension headaches
  • Takes only a minute and needs no equipment

Practice

Hold 20-30 seconds per side, breathing normally. Never force or bounce — the stretch should feel mild, not sharp.
