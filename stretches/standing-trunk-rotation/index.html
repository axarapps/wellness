Standing Trunk Rotation

Spinal mobility, standing

Lower back core

How to

Standing with feet hip-width apart and arms relaxed out to the sides, gently rotate your upper body from side to side, letting your arms swing loosely with the motion.

Benefits

  • A dynamic mobility movement, distinct from a held static stretch
  • Loosens the whole spine and warms up the core before activity
  • Doubles as a quick standing break from sitting

Practice

15-20 slow rotations total, keeping the movement controlled rather than using momentum to swing further.
