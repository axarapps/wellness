Torso Rotation with Arm Swing

Dynamic full-body warm-up

Full body dynamic

How to

Standing with knees soft, rotate your torso side to side while letting your arms swing loosely with the momentum, wrapping gently around your body on each side.

Benefits

  • Warms up the spine, shoulders, and hips together in one dynamic movement
  • A common pre-activity warm-up rather than a static hold
  • Energizing enough to use as a quick midday reset

Practice

20-30 seconds of continuous, relaxed rotation, letting momentum — not force — drive the arm swing.
