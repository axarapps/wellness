Gentle Reclined Twist

Spinal release, wind-down

Wind down

How to

Lying on your back with knees bent, let both knees drop gently to one side while keeping both shoulders resting on the floor, turning your head the opposite way if comfortable.

Benefits

  • A calming, passive twist that releases the lower back and spine
  • Popular as a final wind-down stretch before bed
  • Supporting the knees with a pillow makes it even more restful

Practice

Hold 30-60 seconds per side, letting the weight of your legs do the work rather than pulling them down.
