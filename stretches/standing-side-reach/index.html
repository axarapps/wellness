Standing Side Reach

Full-body side-body stretch

Full body dynamic

How to

Standing with feet hip-width apart, reach one arm straight overhead and lean your torso to the opposite side, feeling a stretch along the whole side of your body from hip to fingertips.

Benefits

  • Stretches the whole side body in one simple movement
  • A good full-body opener to start or end a stretching session
  • Improves the side-bending range of motion used in everyday reaching

Practice

Hold 15-20 seconds per side, keeping hips facing forward rather than twisting toward the reach.
