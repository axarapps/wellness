Standing IT Band Stretch

Outer thigh & hip release

Legs hamstrings

How to

Standing, cross one leg behind the other, then lean your upper body and hips to the side away from the back leg, reaching the same-side arm overhead. A wall nearby can help with balance.

Benefits

  • Targets the iliotibial band along the outer thigh, often overlooked
  • Popular with runners and cyclists for outer-knee and hip discomfort
  • A good complement to hamstring and quad stretches for full leg coverage

Practice

Hold 20-30 seconds per side, easing off if any pinching is felt at the knee rather than a stretch along the thigh.
