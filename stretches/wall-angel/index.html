Wall Angel

Upper back & shoulder mobility

Chest upper back

How to

Stand with your back, head, and arms against a wall, elbows bent at 90 degrees like a goalpost. Slowly slide your arms up the wall as far as they'll go while keeping contact, then slide back down.

Benefits

  • Trains the upper back muscles that posture depends on, not just stretches them
  • Highlights exactly where shoulder mobility is limited
  • A favorite physical-therapy exercise for rounded shoulders

Practice

8-12 slow slides up and down, keeping your lower back flat against the wall throughout.
