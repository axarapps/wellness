Kneeling Hip Flexor Stretch

Hip flexor release, lunge position

Hips glutes

How to

Kneel on one knee with the other foot planted in front, both knees bent near 90 degrees. Keeping your torso upright, shift your weight forward slightly until a stretch is felt at the front of the back hip.

Benefits

  • Directly targets hip flexors shortened by long periods of sitting
  • Tight hip flexors are linked to lower-back discomfort, so this often helps both
  • A cushion or folded towel under the back knee makes it more comfortable

Practice

Hold 20-30 seconds per side, keeping the front knee aligned over the ankle rather than pushing past the toes.
