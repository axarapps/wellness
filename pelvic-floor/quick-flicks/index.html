Quick Flicks

Fast-twitch reps

Pelvic floor

How to

Contract the pelvic floor sharply, as if stopping a sneeze, then release completely. One flick is one second up, one second down.

Benefits

  • Trains the fast-twitch fibers used for coughs, sneezes and sudden laughs
  • Builds control for stress-incontinence moments
  • Pairs well as a warm-up before a long-hold set

Practice

Ten flicks, rest, repeat for three rounds. Speed comes from full release, not from squeezing harder.
