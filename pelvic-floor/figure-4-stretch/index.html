Figure-4 Stretch

Agnistambhasana

Mobility

How to

Sit with one shin stacked on top of the other, both shins roughly parallel, ankles beyond the opposite knees — a seated figure-4 shape.

Benefits

  • Releases the piriformis, a common source of referred pelvic tension
  • Opens the hips and outer thighs
  • Improves the hip rotation that supports better pelvic floor mechanics

Practice

Fold forward gently only as far as the hips allow. Switch which shin is on top between sessions.
