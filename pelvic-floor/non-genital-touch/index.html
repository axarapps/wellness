Non-Genital Touch

Sensate Focus, Stage 1

Sensate focus

How to

Take turns touching each other's body, avoiding breasts and genitals, with no goal beyond noticing sensation. The person touching also pays attention to what they feel.

Benefits

  • The foundational stage of Sensate Focus, a technique developed by Masters and Johnson
  • Removes performance pressure by explicitly ruling out a "goal"
  • Rebuilds comfortable touch as its own experience, not a means to something else

Practice

Agree beforehand on a time limit, and that either person can pause at any point, no explanation needed.
