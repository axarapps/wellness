Deep Squat Hold

Malasana

Mobility

How to

Feet slightly wider than hip-width, lower into a full squat, elbows pressing gently against the inner knees, chest lifted.

Benefits

  • One of the most effective stretches for hip and ankle mobility
  • Encourages a naturally relaxed, lengthened pelvic floor
  • Widely used to prepare the body for labor and pelvic release

Practice

Use a rolled towel under your heels if they lift. Hold and breathe rather than bouncing.
