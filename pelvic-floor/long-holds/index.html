Long Holds

Slow-twitch endurance

Pelvic floor

How to

Contract and lift the pelvic floor gently, hold without straining, then release slowly over the same count.

Benefits

  • Builds endurance in the slow-twitch fibers that hold organs in place all day
  • Improves bladder and bowel control over time
  • Foundation set before adding load (Bridge Kegels)

Practice

Hold for a count of five, release for a count of five. Breathe normally throughout — never hold your breath.
