Reverse Kegels

Deliberate release

Pelvic floor

How to

Instead of lifting, gently bear down and lengthen the pelvic floor — the opposite motion of a contraction — then let it float back to resting length.

Benefits

  • Balances a pelvic floor that's chronically tight, not just weak
  • Often overlooked, but essential for full-range control
  • Can ease tension-related discomfort

Practice

Think "soften and open," not "push." Stop if you feel strain rather than a gentle stretch.
