Lizard Pose

Utthan Pristhasana

Standing

How to

From a low lunge, walk the front foot to the outside of the front hand and lower onto the forearms, letting the hips sink toward the floor.

Benefits

  • Deeply opens the hips and groin
  • Stretches the hip flexors of the back leg
  • Prepares the body for deeper hip-openers

Practice

Hold for 5-8 breaths per side, coming onto the fingertips instead of forearms for a gentler version.
