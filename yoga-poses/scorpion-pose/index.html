Scorpion Pose

Vrischikasana

Inversions

How to

From Forearm Stand, arch the back and bend the knees to bring the feet toward the head, balancing on the forearms in a deep backbend.

Benefits

  • Builds significant shoulder and core strength
  • Deeply opens the spine and shoulders
  • An advanced peak pose combining balance and backbend

Practice

An advanced pose requiring a strong Forearm Stand first — practice near a wall with guidance.
