Revolved Crescent Lunge

Parivrtta Anjaneyasana

Twists

How to

From a low lunge, bring the palms together at the heart and twist to hook the opposite elbow outside the front thigh, back knee lifted.

Benefits

  • Improves spinal rotation while strengthening the legs
  • Stimulates digestion
  • Builds balance and heat

Practice

Hold for 5 breaths per side, keeping the back leg active.
