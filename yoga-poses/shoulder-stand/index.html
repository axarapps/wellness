Shoulder Stand

Sarvangasana

Inversions

How to

From lying down, lift the legs and hips overhead, supporting the lower back with the hands, elbows grounded and shoulder-width apart. Keep the body in one long line from shoulders to feet.

Benefits

  • Stimulates circulation and the thyroid
  • Calms the nervous system
  • Builds shoulder and core strength

Practice

Hold for 30 seconds to a few minutes, building duration gradually. Avoid this pose with neck issues or during menstruation; learn it from a teacher first if new to inversions.
