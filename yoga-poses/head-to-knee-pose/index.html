Head-to-Knee Pose

Janu Sirsasana

Forward folds

How to

Sit with one leg extended and the other foot drawn to the inner thigh, then fold forward over the straight leg, reaching for the foot or shin.

Benefits

  • Stretches the hamstrings and spine
  • Calms the mind
  • Stimulates the abdominal organs

Practice

Hold for 1-2 minutes per side, leading with the chest rather than rounding the back.
