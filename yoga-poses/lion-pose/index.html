Lion Pose

Simhasana

Seated

How to

Kneel with hands on the knees, inhale through the nose, then exhale forcefully through the mouth while sticking out the tongue and opening the eyes wide.

Benefits

  • Releases tension in the face and jaw
  • Stretches the tongue and throat muscles
  • A playful reset for the breath

Practice

Repeat 3-5 times, exhaling fully with each round.
