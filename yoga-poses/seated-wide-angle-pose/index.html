Seated Wide-Angle Pose

Upavistha Konasana

Forward folds

How to

Sit with the legs spread wide, flex the feet, and walk the hands forward between the legs, folding from the hips.

Benefits

  • Stretches the inner thighs and hamstrings
  • Improves hip mobility
  • Calms the nervous system

Practice

Hold for 1-3 minutes, keeping the spine long rather than collapsing into the fold.
