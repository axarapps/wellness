Crow Pose

Bakasana

Balance

How to

Squat with the hands planted shoulder-width apart, knees resting on the backs of the upper arms, then shift the weight forward until the feet lift off the floor.

Benefits

  • Builds arm and core strength
  • Improves balance and focus
  • Builds confidence

Practice

Practice near a cushion while learning; hold for a few breaths as balance develops.
