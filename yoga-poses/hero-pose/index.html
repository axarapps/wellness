Hero Pose

Virasana

Seated

How to

Kneel with the knees together and feet slightly wider than the hips, then sit back between the heels, keeping the spine tall.

Benefits

  • Stretches the thighs and ankles
  • Improves posture
  • A steady seat for breathing practice

Practice

Sit for 1-5 minutes; use a block under the sitting bones if the hips don't reach the floor comfortably.
