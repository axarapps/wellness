Half Standing Forward Bend

Ardha Uttanasana

Forward folds

How to

From Standing Forward Bend, lift the torso halfway, lengthening the spine flat and placing the fingertips on the floor, shins, or blocks.

Benefits

  • Builds core and back strength
  • Improves spinal alignment
  • A useful transition between standing poses

Practice

Hold for a breath or two as a transition, or 5 breaths as a standalone pose.
