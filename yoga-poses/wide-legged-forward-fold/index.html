Wide-Legged Forward Fold

Prasarita Padottanasana

Forward folds

How to

Step the feet wide apart, hinge from the hips and fold forward, placing the hands on the floor or a block beneath the shoulders.

Benefits

  • Stretches the hamstrings and inner thighs
  • Calms the nervous system
  • Relieves mild backache

Practice

Hold for 5-8 breaths, letting the crown of the head release toward the floor.
