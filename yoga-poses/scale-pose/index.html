Scale Pose

Tulasana

Balance

How to

From a cross-legged seat, place the palms flat on the floor beside the hips and press down to lift the entire body, legs and all, off the floor.

Benefits

  • Builds significant arm and shoulder strength
  • Strengthens the wrists
  • A traditional finishing pose for a seated sequence

Practice

Hold for a few breaths, building duration gradually as strength develops.
