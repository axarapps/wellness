Locust Pose

Salabhasana

Backbends

How to

Lie face down with arms alongside the body, palms down, then lift the chest, arms and legs away from the floor together, gazing forward.

Benefits

  • Strengthens the entire back body
  • Improves posture
  • Stimulates digestion

Practice

Hold for 3-5 breaths, then rest. Repeat 2-3 times.
