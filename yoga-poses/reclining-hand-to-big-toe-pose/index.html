Reclining Hand-to-Big-Toe Pose

Supta Padangusthasana

Restorative

How to

Lie on your back, extend one leg toward the ceiling and hold the big toe or a strap around the foot, keeping the other leg grounded.

Benefits

  • Stretches the hamstrings gently
  • Relieves lower back tension
  • Easy to hold for longer periods

Practice

Hold for 1-2 minutes per side, keeping the raised leg only as straight as is comfortable.
