Half Moon Pose

Ardha Chandrasana

Balance

How to

From Triangle Pose, shift the weight onto the front hand and standing leg, lifting the back leg parallel to the floor and opening the hips toward the sky.

Benefits

  • Strengthens the standing leg and core
  • Improves balance and coordination
  • Opens the hips and chest

Practice

Hold for 5 breaths per side, using a block under the bottom hand while building balance.
