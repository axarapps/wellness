Pyramid Pose

Parsvottanasana

Forward folds

How to

From a short stance with the front leg straight, square the hips and fold forward over the front leg, hands framing the foot or resting on blocks.

Benefits

  • Stretches the hamstrings and calves
  • Improves balance and hip alignment
  • Calms the mind

Practice

Hold for 5-8 breaths per side, keeping the spine long rather than rounding to reach further.
