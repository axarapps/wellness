Revolved Wide-Legged Forward Fold

Parivrtta Prasarita Padottanasana

Twists

How to

From a wide standing straddle, fold forward and twist to reach one hand toward the opposite foot, extending the other arm toward the ceiling.

Benefits

  • Combines a hamstring stretch with spinal rotation
  • Improves balance and coordination
  • Stimulates digestion

Practice

Hold for 5 breaths per side, keeping the legs active and straight.
