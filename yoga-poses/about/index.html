About Yoga Poses

Asanas is a calm, focused guide to 100 yoga poses and asanas — built for anyone who wants clear instruction without the noise of video ads or a cluttered feed.

In the Asanas app

  • 100 yoga poses across the eight traditional asana families — standing, seated, twists, backbends, forward folds, inversions, balance, and restorative
  • Clear technique, benefits, and step-by-step practice notes for every pose, written in plain language
  • A circular practice timer — drag the dial to set your hold time, or start straight from the last one you used
  • Favorites, so your go-to poses are always one tap away
  • A practice journal that quietly logs what you've held and for how long, so you can see your practice build over time
  • Full light and dark themes, with artwork that adapts to both
  • Browse by family, or search by name or benefit ("hip opener," "core," "calming"...)
