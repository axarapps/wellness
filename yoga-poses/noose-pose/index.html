Noose Pose

Pasasana

Twists

How to

From a low squat with feet together, twist the torso to hook one elbow outside the opposite knee, wrapping the arms around the shins if possible.

Benefits

  • Deeply wrings the spine and abdomen
  • Improves ankle and hip mobility
  • Aids digestion

Practice

An advanced twist — hold for a few breaths per side, coming out slowly if the heels lift too much.
