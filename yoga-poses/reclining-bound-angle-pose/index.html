Reclining Bound Angle Pose

Supta Baddha Konasana

Restorative

How to

Lie on your back with the soles of the feet together and knees falling open to the sides, arms resting open at your sides.

Benefits

  • Opens the hips gently
  • Calms the nervous system
  • Relieves tension without effort

Practice

Hold for 3-10 minutes, supporting the knees with cushions if needed.
