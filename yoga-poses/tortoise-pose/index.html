Tortoise Pose

Kurmasana

Forward folds

How to

Sit with the legs wide, bend the knees, and slide the arms under the knees before straightening the legs and lowering the torso between them.

Benefits

  • Deeply stretches the hips, shoulders and spine
  • Calms the mind through a long, held fold
  • A traditional pose for turning attention inward

Practice

An advanced fold — build toward it with hamstring and shoulder openers first, and hold for as long as feels steady.
