Upward-Facing Dog

Urdhva Mukha Svanasana

Backbends

How to

From lying face down, press through the hands to straighten the arms, lifting the chest and thighs off the floor while the tops of the feet press down.

Benefits

  • Strengthens the arms, spine and wrists
  • Opens the chest and shoulders
  • Energizes the body

Practice

Hold for 3-5 breaths, keeping the shoulders drawing away from the ears.
