Happy Baby Pose

Ananda Balasana

Restorative

How to

Lie on your back, draw the knees toward the chest and hold the outer edges of the feet, gently rocking side to side.

Benefits

  • Releases tension in the hips and lower back
  • Calms the mind
  • A playful, easy stretch

Practice

Hold for 5-10 breaths, keeping the lower back grounded.
