Inverted Staff Pose

Viparita Dandasana

Inversions

How to

From a supported headstand or backbend setup, arch the spine so the crown of the head and forearms support the body in a deep backward arch.

Benefits

  • Combines the benefits of backbends and inversions
  • Opens the chest and shoulders deeply
  • An advanced pose usually built up to gradually

Practice

An advanced pose best learned with a teacher; not a beginner inversion.
