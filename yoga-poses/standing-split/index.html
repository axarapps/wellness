Standing Split

Urdhva Prasarita Eka Padasana

Standing

How to

From a standing forward fold, shift weight onto one leg and lift the other straight up behind you, hands framing the standing foot.

Benefits

  • Stretches the hamstrings deeply on both legs
  • Improves balance
  • Strengthens the standing leg

Practice

Hold for 5 breaths per side, keeping the hips square rather than letting the lifted leg twist open.
