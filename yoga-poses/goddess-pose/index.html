Goddess Pose

Utkata Konasana

Standing

How to

Step the feet wide with toes turned out, bend the knees over the ankles, and bring the arms into a cactus shape at shoulder height.

Benefits

  • Strengthens the legs and glutes
  • Opens the hips and groin
  • Builds lower-body stamina

Practice

Hold for 5-10 breaths, keeping the tailbone drawing down and the knees tracking over the toes.
