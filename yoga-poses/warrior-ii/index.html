Warrior II

Virabhadrasana II

Standing

How to

From a wide stance, turn the right foot out 90 degrees and the left foot slightly in. Bend the right knee over the right ankle, extend the arms parallel to the floor, and gaze past the right fingertips.

Benefits

  • Builds strength in the legs and core
  • Opens the hips and chest
  • Improves focus and stamina

Practice

Hold for 5-8 breaths per side. Keep the front knee tracking over the ankle, not past it.
