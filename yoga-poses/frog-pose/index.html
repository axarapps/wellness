Frog Pose

Bhekasana

Backbends

How to

Lie face down, bend both knees and reach back to hold the feet, drawing the heels toward the hips while lifting the chest.

Benefits

  • Stretches the quadriceps and hip flexors deeply
  • Opens the chest and shoulders
  • Improves knee flexibility

Practice

Hold for 3-5 breaths, releasing immediately if there's any knee strain.
