Eagle Pose

Garudasana

Balance

How to

Cross the right thigh over the left, hooking the right foot behind the left calf if possible. Cross the left arm over the right and press the palms together, then sink the hips back slightly.

Benefits

  • Improves balance and concentration
  • Stretches the shoulders and upper back
  • Strengthens the ankles

Practice

Hold for 5 breaths per side. Keep the standing knee soft, not locked.
