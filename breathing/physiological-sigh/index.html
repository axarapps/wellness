Physiological Sigh

Double inhale, long exhale

Reset

The pattern

Inhale 2 · Hold 1 · Exhale 6. Repeat for about 3 rounds.

Why it helps

A double inhale re-inflates collapsed air sacs in the lungs, and the long exhale is the fastest known way to lower physiological arousal in the moment.

How to do it

Take a deep inhale through the nose, then a second short sharp inhale on top of it, then let out one long, slow exhale through the mouth.

Good for

  • Fast stress reset
  • Between hard tasks
  • Calming down quickly
