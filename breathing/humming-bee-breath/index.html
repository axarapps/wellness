Humming Bee Breath

Bhramari

Reset

The pattern

Inhale 4 · Exhale 6. Repeat for about 6 rounds.

Why it helps

The gentle vibration from humming on the exhale is a fast, embodied way to interrupt spiraling thoughts.

How to do it

Inhale through the nose, then exhale slowly through the nose while making a steady humming sound, like a bee.

Good for

  • Interrupting racing thoughts
  • Tension headaches
  • A private, portable reset
