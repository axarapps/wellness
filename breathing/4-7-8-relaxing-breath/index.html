4-7-8 Relaxing Breath

Dr. Andrew Weil's technique

Wind down

The pattern

Inhale 4 · Hold 7 · Exhale 8. Repeat for about 4 rounds.

Why it helps

The long, slow exhale relative to the inhale is thought to activate the parasympathetic nervous system, making this a popular technique for falling asleep.

How to do it

Inhale quietly through the nose for 4 counts, hold for 7, then exhale completely through the mouth for 8, making a soft whoosh sound.

Good for

  • Winding down before sleep
  • Easing anxiety
  • Slowing a racing mind
