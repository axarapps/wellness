Extended Exhale

Exhale longer than you inhale

Wind down

The pattern

Inhale 4 · Exhale 8. Repeat for about 6 rounds.

Why it helps

A longer exhale than inhale is one of the simplest, most reliable ways to shift the body out of a stressed state.

How to do it

Inhale through the nose for 4 counts, then exhale slowly through the nose or mouth for 8 counts. No holding.

Good for

  • General anxiety
  • Before bed
  • Calming a racing heart
