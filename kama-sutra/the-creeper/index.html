The Creeper

Lataveshtitaka

Standing

How to

Standing embrace with one leg wrapped around the partner's hip for support, weight shared between both.

Benefits

  • Full-body closeness
  • Builds trust in shared balance
  • A playful change from lying down

Practice

The standing partner can brace against a wall — agree on it beforehand so neither of you is guessing.
