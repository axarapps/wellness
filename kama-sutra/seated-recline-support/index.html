Seated Recline Support

Asraya Asana

Seated

How to

One partner sits with back supported by pillows or a headboard, the other leans in close, seated or kneeling in front.

Benefits

  • Comfortable for longer sessions thanks to back support
  • Reduces strain on the supported partner
  • Good for slower, more relaxed pacing

Practice

Stack pillows generously — good support is what makes this one comfortable enough to linger in.
