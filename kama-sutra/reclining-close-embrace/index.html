Reclining Close Embrace

Alingana Sayana

Reclining

How to

Both lie on their sides or backs, pulled as close together as possible, arms wrapped fully around each other.

Benefits

  • Maximum skin contact with minimal movement
  • Very calm and unhurried
  • A good position for winding down together

Practice

Sometimes the point is just to stay still and close — this one doesn't need to lead anywhere else.
