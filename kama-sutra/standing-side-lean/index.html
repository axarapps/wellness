Standing Side Lean

Parsva Sthiti

Standing

How to

Both stand side by side, turned slightly toward each other, leaning in for a close embrace.

Benefits

  • Gentle and unhurried
  • Good for building anticipation
  • Comfortable for a long, slow embrace

Practice

There's no rush here — this one rewards taking your time before anything else happens.
