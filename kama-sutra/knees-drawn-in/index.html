Knees Drawn In

Janu Akarshana

Reclining

How to

One partner lies back with knees drawn toward the chest, the other kneels close in front.

Benefits

  • A deeper, closer angle for some couples
  • Good variation from flat reclining
  • Comfortable for the partner being held

Practice

Knees-to-chest isn't comfortable for everyone's hips or back — ease into it and back off if anything pulls.
