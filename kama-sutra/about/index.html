About Kama Sutra

Kama Sutra is a calm, tasteful guide to 100 Kama Sutra positions for couples — written in plain language and illustrated as simple abstract line art rather than photos, so it's something you'd actually be comfortable opening together.

In the Kama Sutra app

  • 100 positions across five themes — standing, seated, reclining, from behind, and side-by-side
  • A short connection tip, technique, and "good for" note for every position, written with real couples in mind, not clinical jargon
  • "Surprise Me" for when you'd rather let chance choose
  • A Journey tab that quietly tracks what you've tried — by theme, in curated collections, and a wall of stamps — with no pressure, no streaks
  • Favorites, so the ones you love are always one tap away
  • Full light and dark themes
  • Search by name or mood ("close," "playful," "unhurried"...)
