Facing Union

Samapada

Standing

How to

Standing, facing each other, close enough to embrace. Works well against a wall or headboard for support.

Benefits

  • Eye contact comes easily here
  • Good for slowing down and checking in
  • Playful with a height difference

Practice

Say what feels good out loud — standing positions make it easy to pause and adjust together.
