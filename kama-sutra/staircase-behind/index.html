Staircase Behind

Sopana Paschat

From behind

How to

Using a staircase to adjust height, one partner a step down, standing close behind the other.

Benefits

  • Solves height differences naturally, no props needed
  • A playful change of setting
  • Easy to hold the railing for balance

Practice

Same caution as any staircase position — solid footing first, everything else second.
