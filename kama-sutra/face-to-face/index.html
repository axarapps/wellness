Face to Face

Sammukha Asana

Reclining

How to

Both lie facing each other, close together, one partner's leg often resting over the other's hip.

Benefits

  • The most classic reclining closeness
  • Easy eye contact and kissing throughout
  • Comfortable for a long, unhurried pace

Practice

A simple, easy starting point — good for slowing down and reconnecting before trying anything more elaborate.
