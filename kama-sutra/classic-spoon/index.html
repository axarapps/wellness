Classic Spoon

Parsva Sama

Side by side

How to

Both lie on their sides, facing the same direction, bodies pulled close together.

Benefits

  • Very low effort, comfortable for a long time
  • Good for half-asleep, unhurried moments
  • Easy to stay close afterward without moving

Practice

Often the easiest position to just talk during — there's no pressure to face each other.
