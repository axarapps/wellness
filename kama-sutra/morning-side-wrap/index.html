Morning Side Wrap

Prabhata Veshtana

Side by side

How to

A slow, half-asleep version of the side embrace, minimal movement, bodies close from waking up together.

Benefits

  • Gentle and unhurried, needing very little effort
  • A lovely way to start the day close together
  • No pressure for it to lead anywhere further

Practice

Sometimes the best version of this app's whole point is just staying still together a little longer.
