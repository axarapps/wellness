Deep Fold

Nata Asana

Reclining

How to

One partner lies back with hips lifted and knees brought toward the chest and head, a flexible variation; the other kneels close in front.

Benefits

  • A more intense angle for couples who enjoy it
  • A good stretch for the folded partner beforehand helps
  • Very close, deep contact

Practice

This needs real flexibility and a slow warm-up — skip it if it strains rather than stretches.
