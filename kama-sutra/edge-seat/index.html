Edge Seat

Prantasana

Seated

How to

One partner sits at the edge of a bed or sturdy chair, the other kneels or stands close in front.

Benefits

  • Comfortable height for the standing or kneeling partner
  • Easy to maintain eye contact
  • Simple to set up anywhere with a sturdy edge

Practice

Check the edge is stable — a wobbly mattress edge is more distracting than romantic.
