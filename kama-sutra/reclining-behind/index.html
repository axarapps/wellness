Reclining Behind

Sayana Paschat

From behind

How to

The front partner lies on their side, the other close behind and slightly above, one leg often draped over.

Benefits

  • Relaxed and low-effort for both
  • Comfortable for a longer, slower pace
  • Easy to stay close afterward

Practice

A gentle, unhurried option — good for when neither of you wants to do much moving.
