Seated Lap Behind

Anka Paschat

From behind

How to

One partner sits, the other sits on their lap facing away, leaning back into their chest.

Benefits

  • Close body contact with hands free for the back partner
  • A gentler angle than standing or kneeling versions
  • Comfortable for a slower pace

Practice

Good for a lazy, unhurried session — let the seated partner's chest support some of the weight.
