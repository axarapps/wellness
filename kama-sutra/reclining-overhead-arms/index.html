Reclining Overhead Arms

Urdhva Bahu Sayana

Reclining

How to

Reclining with arms stretched overhead, an open, relaxed posture, the other close in front.

Benefits

  • An open, exposed-feeling posture some couples enjoy
  • A different visual and physical sensation
  • Easy to combine with other reclining positions

Practice

This one is about the open feeling as much as anything — let it be a stretch, not a strain.
