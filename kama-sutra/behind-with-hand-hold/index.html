Behind with Hand-Hold

Kara Bandha Paschat

From behind

How to

Kneeling close together, one behind the other, hands clasped together rather than resting on the hips.

Benefits

  • Adds an intimate detail to a kneeling position
  • Keeps a sense of connection throughout
  • Simple to add to most kneeling positions

Practice

A small change — clasped hands instead of hips — that a lot of couples find makes it feel less impersonal.
