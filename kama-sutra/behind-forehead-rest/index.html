Behind Forehead Rest

Lalata Vishranti Paschat

From behind

How to

The front partner rests their forehead on folded arms, kneeling, while the other stays close behind, slow and gentle.

Benefits

  • A relaxed, low-effort variation
  • Comfortable for a longer, slower pace
  • Good for a tired-but-still-close mood

Practice

This one leans gentle and slow by design — no need to rush it.
