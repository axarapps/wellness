Lap Facing

Anka Asana

Seated

How to

One partner sits, the other sits on their lap facing them, arms wrapped around each other.

Benefits

  • Very close, face-to-face contact
  • Easy to kiss and talk throughout
  • Comfortable for a long, slow pace

Practice

Great for eye contact — let the closeness slow things down rather than speed them up.
