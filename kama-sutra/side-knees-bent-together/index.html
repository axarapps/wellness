Side Knees Bent Together

Parsva Janu

Side by side

How to

Lying on their sides facing each other, knees bent and touching, close and relaxed.

Benefits

  • A small, comfortable detail added to side-facing closeness
  • Easy on the hips and back
  • Comfortable to hold for a while

Practice

A gentle, low-effort variation — good for winding down at the end of the day.
