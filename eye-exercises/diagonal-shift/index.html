Diagonal Shift

Looking corner to corner, both directions

Movement tracking

How to

Without moving your head, look up toward one top corner of your vision, then diagonally down to the opposite bottom corner. Hold briefly at each end, then repeat along the other diagonal.

Benefits

  • Completes the set of movement directions alongside horizontal and vertical shifts
  • A short, varied close to a full eye-movement routine
  • Can reveal a diagonal that feels noticeably stiffer than the rest

Practice

5 repetitions along each diagonal.
