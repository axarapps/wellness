Up-and-Down Shift

Looking fully up, then fully down

Movement tracking

How to

Without moving your head, look as far up as comfortable, hold briefly, then look as far down as comfortable, hold briefly. Move smoothly between the two rather than jumping.

Benefits

  • Complements Side-to-Side Shift for full vertical range
  • Targets a direction of movement almost never used during typing or reading
  • Gentle enough to repeat several times through the day

Practice

Repeat the up-down shift 10 times.
