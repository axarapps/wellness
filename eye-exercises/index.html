Eye Exercises

14 exercises. Eye exercises to rest tired eyes during screen time: blinking, focus shifts, palming and more.

Warmup blinking

  • Rapid BlinkingQuick, full blinks to refresh the surface
  • PalmingCupped, warmed hands over closed eyes

Focus training

  • Near-Far Focus ShiftAlternating focus between a close and a distant point
  • Pencil Push-UpsTracking a pencil as it moves toward your nose
  • ZoomingThumb moving from arm's length to near focus

Movement tracking

  • Eye RotationSlow circles around the full range of vision
  • Figure-8 TracingTracing an imaginary sideways figure-8
  • Side-to-Side ShiftLooking fully left, then fully right
  • Up-and-Down ShiftLooking fully up, then fully down
  • Diagonal ShiftLooking corner to corner, both directions

Relaxation care

  • TratakaSteady, unblinking gaze at a fixed point
  • SunningClosed eyes turned toward gentle sunlight
  • The 20-20-20 BreakEvery 20 minutes, look 20 feet away for 20 seconds
  • Gentle Eye MassageLight circular pressure around the eye socket
