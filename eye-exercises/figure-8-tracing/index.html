Figure-8 Tracing

Tracing an imaginary sideways figure-8

Movement tracking

How to

Picture a large figure-8 lying on its side, about 10 feet in front of you. Trace its outline slowly with your eyes alone, without moving your head, following the loop smoothly in one direction, then the other.

Benefits

  • Combines horizontal, vertical, and diagonal tracking in one smooth motion
  • A more varied path than simple side-to-side or up-down movement
  • Often used in vision-therapy and sports-vision training routines

Practice

Trace the figure-8 slowly 5 times in each direction.
