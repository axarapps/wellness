Gentle Eye Massage

Light circular pressure around the eye socket

Relaxation care

How to

Using your ring fingers, apply very light pressure in slow circles along the bone of the eye socket — under the brow, along the temple, and under the eye — never on the eyeball itself. Keep the pressure gentle throughout.

Benefits

  • Eases tension in the muscles surrounding the eyes, not the eyes directly
  • A calming way to close out an eye-care routine
  • Pairs naturally with slow, deep breathing

Practice

30-45 seconds of gentle circles, breathing slowly throughout.
