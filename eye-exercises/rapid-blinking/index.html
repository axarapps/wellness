Rapid Blinking

Quick, full blinks to refresh the surface

Warmup blinking

How to

Sit comfortably and blink quickly but gently about 10 times in a row — a light flutter, not a squeeze. Then close your eyes and rest for 20 seconds before opening them again.

Benefits

  • Spreads fresh tear film across the eye's surface
  • A fast reset for the dry, gritty feeling of a long screen session
  • Takes under a minute, no equipment needed

Practice

Repeat the 10-blink, 20-second-rest cycle 2-3 times.
