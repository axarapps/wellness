Side-to-Side Shift

Looking fully left, then fully right

Movement tracking

How to

Without moving your head, look as far left as comfortable, hold briefly, then look as far right as comfortable, hold briefly. Keep the motion smooth rather than snapping between the two sides.

Benefits

  • A simple, low-effort way to move the eyes through a range screens rarely require
  • Easy to do discreetly while seated anywhere
  • A good warm-up before more demanding tracking exercises

Practice

Repeat the left-right shift 10 times.
