Near-Far Focus Shift

Alternating focus between a close and a distant point

Focus training

How to

Hold a thumb at arm's length and focus on it clearly for a few seconds. Then shift your focus to an object 15-20 feet away, holding that focus for a few seconds too. Alternate slowly back and forth.

Benefits

  • Retrains the eye's focusing muscle after long stretches of only near focus
  • A close relative of the eye-doctor-recommended 20-20-20 habit
  • Simple to do at a desk with nothing but your own thumb

Practice

Repeat the near-far alternation 10 times, breathing normally.
