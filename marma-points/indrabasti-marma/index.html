Indrabasti Marma

Middle of the forearm

Arms and elbows

How to

Using the thumb of the opposite hand, press firmly into the fleshy middle of your forearm, roughly halfway between your wrist and elbow, on the inner side.

Benefits

  • A traditional point associated with settling nausea and unease
  • Useful for general forearm tension from typing or lifting
  • Simple to press discreetly while seated anywhere

Practice

Press 30-60 seconds per arm.
