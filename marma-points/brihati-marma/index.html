Brihati Marma

Mid-back, between the shoulder blades

Back

How to

Using a tennis ball or similar firm object against a wall, or the fingers of both hands reaching back, press into the muscle between your shoulder blades. Hold with steady pressure.

Benefits

  • A common spot for tension that's hard to reach unassisted
  • Often felt immediately after long periods of sitting
  • A wall or a partner's help can make this point easier to reach fully

Practice

Press for 30-60 seconds, using a wall or tool if reaching by hand is difficult.
