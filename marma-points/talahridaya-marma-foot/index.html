Talahridaya Marma (Foot)

Center of the sole

Feet and legs

How to

Press your thumb firmly into the center of the sole of your foot, in the soft hollow just below the ball of the foot. Hold with steady pressure, or massage in slow circles.

Benefits

  • A grounding point, often used to ease anxiety and restlessness
  • A satisfying point to work after a long day on your feet
  • Easy to reach seated, with or without a massage tool

Practice

Press or circle for 45-60 seconds per foot.
