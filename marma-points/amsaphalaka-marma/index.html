Amsaphalaka Marma

Shoulder blade

Neck and shoulders

How to

Reach across your body and press your fingers into the muscle along the inner edge of your shoulder blade, as far as you can comfortably reach. Hold, or use small circular motions.

Benefits

  • Targets tension that builds from rounded, forward-leaning posture
  • A useful counter-point to Amsa Marma for a fuller shoulder routine
  • Can be reached more fully by leaning into a wall or doorframe

Practice

Press or circle for 30-60 seconds per side, or lean into a wall to deepen the pressure.
