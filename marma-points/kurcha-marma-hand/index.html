Kurcha Marma (Hand)

Base of the thumb

Hands and wrists

How to

Press firmly into the fleshy mound at the base of your thumb, on the palm side, using the thumb of your opposite hand. Hold, or work in slow circles across the whole mound.

Benefits

  • Targets a muscle group used constantly for gripping and typing
  • Useful after repetitive hand use, such as writing or device use
  • Easy to combine with a gentle stretch of the thumb itself

Practice

Press 30-60 seconds per hand.
