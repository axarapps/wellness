Sthapani Marma

Between the eyebrows

Head and crown

How to

Press one or two fingers gently into the point directly between your eyebrows, just above the bridge of the nose. Hold with light, steady pressure.

Benefits

  • Known traditionally as the "third eye" point, used for mental clarity
  • A common point for easing tension headaches
  • Calming enough to pair with slow breathing as a brief pause

Practice

Press 30-60 seconds, breathing slowly and evenly.
