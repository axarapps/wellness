Parshva Sandhi Marma

Side of the torso, at the ribs

Back

How to

Reach one arm overhead and use the opposite hand to press into the side of your ribcage, just below the armpit. Hold, or work in small circles along the side body.

Benefits

  • Targets a rarely stretched area along the side of the torso
  • Can ease tightness that contributes to shallow breathing
  • Pairs naturally with a gentle side-bending stretch

Practice

Press or circle for 30-45 seconds per side.
