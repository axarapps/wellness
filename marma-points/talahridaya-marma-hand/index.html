Talahridaya Marma (Hand)

Center of the palm

Hands and wrists

How to

Press the thumb of the opposite hand firmly into the center of your palm, where a loosely closed fist would naturally touch. Hold with steady pressure, or massage in small circles.

Benefits

  • A grounding point, often used to ease anxiety or a racing mind
  • Simple enough to do one-handed while seated anywhere
  • Pairs naturally with a slow, deliberate breath

Practice

Press 30-60 seconds per hand, breathing slowly throughout.
