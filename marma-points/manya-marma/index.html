Manya Marma

Side of the neck

Neck and shoulders

How to

Place two or three fingers on the side of your neck, along the large muscle that runs from behind the ear down to the collarbone. Press gently and hold, or use slow strokes downward.

Benefits

  • Eases tension that builds up from screen time and stress
  • A common point to work before or after a neck stretch
  • Gentle downward strokes can feel calming on their own

Practice

Press or stroke for 30-60 seconds per side, using gentle pressure — avoid pressing on the front of the throat.
