Kshipra Marma (Foot)

Between the big toe and second toe

Feet and legs

How to

Using your thumb, press firmly into the webbing between your big toe and second toe, at the top of the foot. Hold with steady pressure, or work in small circles.

Benefits

  • A traditional point associated with easing tension and stress
  • Simple to do seated, with no need to remove more than a shoe and sock
  • A good complement to the hand version of this same point

Practice

Press 30-60 seconds per foot.
