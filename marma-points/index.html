Marma Points

28 points. Marma points from Ayurveda: where each point is, how to press or massage it gently and what it is traditionally used for.

Hands and wrists

  • Kshipra Marma (Hand)Between thumb and index finger
  • Talahridaya Marma (Hand)Center of the palm
  • Manibandha MarmaInner wrist crease
  • Kurcha Marma (Hand)Base of the thumb

Head and crown

  • Shankha MarmaThe temple
  • Sthapani MarmaBetween the eyebrows
  • Adhipati MarmaCrown of the head
  • Utkshepa MarmaUpper skull, above the ear

Face

  • Apanga MarmaOuter corner of the eye
  • Ganda MarmaThe cheekbone
  • Nasa MarmaBeside the nostrils

Neck and shoulders

  • Manya MarmaSide of the neck
  • Krikatika MarmaBase of the skull, nape of the neck
  • Amsa MarmaTop of the shoulder
  • Amsaphalaka MarmaShoulder blade

Arms and elbows

  • Kurpara MarmaThe elbow crease
  • Indrabasti MarmaMiddle of the forearm
  • Bahvi MarmaUpper arm, the bicep

Abdomen and chest

  • Nabhi MarmaThe navel
  • Hridaya MarmaCenter of the chest
  • Basti MarmaLower abdomen

Back

  • Kukundara MarmaLower back, above the hips
  • Parshva Sandhi MarmaSide of the torso, at the ribs
  • Brihati MarmaMid-back, between the shoulder blades

Feet and legs

  • Kshipra Marma (Foot)Between the big toe and second toe
  • Talahridaya Marma (Foot)Center of the sole
  • Gulpha MarmaThe ankle
  • Janu MarmaThe knee
