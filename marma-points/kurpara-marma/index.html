Kurpara Marma

The elbow crease

Arms and elbows

How to

With your arm slightly bent, press your thumb into the crease on the inner side of your elbow, toward the outer edge near the forearm bone. Hold with steady pressure.

Benefits

  • A traditional point associated with the arm and forearm muscles
  • Useful after repetitive arm movements or heavy lifting
  • Simple to combine with a gentle forearm stretch

Practice

Press 30-45 seconds per arm.
