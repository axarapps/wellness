Nasa Marma

Beside the nostrils

Face

How to

Press a fingertip gently into the small groove on either side of your nose, level with the nostrils. Hold with steady pressure, or massage in tiny circles.

Benefits

  • A traditional point for easing sinus pressure and stuffiness
  • Gentle enough to do frequently through the day
  • Pairs naturally with slow breathing through the nose

Practice

Press 20-30 seconds per side, breathing slowly.
