Basti Marma

Lower abdomen

Abdomen and chest

How to

Place your palm flat on your lower abdomen, a few finger-widths below your navel. Press gently and hold, or massage in slow circles.

Benefits

  • A traditional point associated with the lower body and elimination
  • Calming when paired with slow, deliberate breathing
  • Gentle enough to use daily as part of a wind-down routine

Practice

Press or circle gently for 30-60 seconds, breathing slowly throughout.
