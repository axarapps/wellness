Apanga Marma

Outer corner of the eye

Face

How to

Rest a fingertip lightly in the small hollow just beyond the outer corner of each eye, on the bone of the eye socket. Press very gently and hold.

Benefits

  • A gentle point for easing tension around tired eyes
  • Often used alongside Shankha Marma for eye strain
  • Requires only the lightest touch to feel effective

Practice

Press very gently for 20-30 seconds — this area is delicate and close to the eye.
