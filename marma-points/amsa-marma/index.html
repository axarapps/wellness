Amsa Marma

Top of the shoulder

Neck and shoulders

How to

Reach across with the opposite hand and squeeze the meaty muscle at the top of your shoulder, between your neck and the point of your shoulder. Hold with firm pressure, or knead gently.

Benefits

  • One of the most common places to carry stress and tension
  • Easy to reach and self-treat without any tools
  • Effective combined with a few slow shoulder rolls afterward

Practice

Squeeze or knead for 30-60 seconds per side.
