Utkshepa Marma

Upper skull, above the ear

Head and crown

How to

Place your fingers on the skull just above and slightly behind the top of your ear. Press firmly, or massage in small circles across the area.

Benefits

  • Useful for tension that concentrates on one side of the head
  • Easy to combine with Shankha Marma for a fuller head routine
  • Simple enough to do one-handed while seated anywhere

Practice

Press or circle for 30-60 seconds per side.
