Kataka mudra

The link

How to

Curl your index finger and thumb into a loose circle while the other fingers stay gently curved, as though holding a delicate flower.

Benefits

  • Encourages gentleness and openness
  • Softens a defensive mind
  • Supports graceful movement

Practice

Hold loosely during light movement or a dance-based practice.
