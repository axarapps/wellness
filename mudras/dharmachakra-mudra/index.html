Dharmachakra mudra

Turning the wheel

Heart & circulation

How to

Form Vitarka mudra with both hands, then touch the circle of your right hand's thumb and index finger to the open palm of your left hand at chest height.

Benefits

  • Supports focus during study or teaching
  • Encourages the flow of ideas
  • Balances give and take

Practice

Hold at the heart while contemplating a teaching or intention.
