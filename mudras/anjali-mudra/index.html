Anjali mudra

Salutation

How to

Bring both palms together at the center of your chest, fingers pointing upward, thumbs resting lightly against the sternum.

Benefits

  • Centers and balances the mind before meditation
  • Encourages a sense of gratitude and calm
  • Relieves everyday stress

Practice

Hold for a few calm breaths at the start or end of any practice.
