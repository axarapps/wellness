Bhairava mudra

Fierce stillness

How to

Rest the back of your right hand in the palm of your left hand, both palms facing upward, resting together in your lap.

Benefits

  • Settles a restless mind
  • Encourages introspection
  • Balances the left and right sides of the body

Practice

Hold in the lap throughout seated meditation.
