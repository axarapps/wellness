Yoni mudra

Source

How to

Interlace your fingers, tuck the middle fingers together to form a downward point, then extend the index fingers and thumbs so their tips touch.

Benefits

  • Turns attention inward for deep meditation
  • Balances masculine and feminine energy
  • Calms the mind

Practice

Hold at the level of the lower abdomen during seated meditation.
