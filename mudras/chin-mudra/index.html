Chin mudra

Consciousness

Stress & anxietyCalm & meditation

How to

Touch the tip of your index finger to the tip of your thumb, extend the other three fingers, and rest the hand palm-down on your knee.

Benefits

  • Grounds and calms the nervous system
  • Encourages receptivity
  • Deepens meditation

Practice

Hold for the duration of a seated meditation.
