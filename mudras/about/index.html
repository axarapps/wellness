About Mudras

Mudras is an illustrated hand mudra guide for yoga and meditation — a field journal for the hands. Hand mudras are finger positions used to focus the mind and direct the body's energy.

In the Mudras app

  • Dozens of hand mudras, from well-known yoga mudras like Gyan and Anjali to lesser-known ones like Ksepana and Kalesvara
  • Technique, benefits, and practice notes for every mudra
  • Categories grouped by what they're traditionally used for — stress, sleep, digestion, heart and circulation, and more
  • A practice timer with a gentle completion chime, so you can hold a mudra for exactly as long as you intend to
  • A Mudra of the Day, to give your practice a starting point
  • A Journal that tracks your practice streak and collects a stamp for every mudra you complete
  • Weekly Collections — themed sets of mudras for calm, sleep, the heart, energy, and stress
  • An entirely optional daily reminder, only if you turn it on — no notifications otherwise
  • Favorites, so the gestures you return to are always one tap away
  • A short introduction to the tradition behind mudras — what they are, how they're practiced, and the five elements (earth, water, fire, air, and space) the fingers are traditionally said to correspond to
