Akash mudra

The sky

How to

Touch the tip of your middle finger to the tip of your thumb, keeping the other fingers extended. Do this with each hand.

Benefits

  • Relieves heart pain and palpitations
  • Strengthens bones and teeth
  • Improves hearing

Practice

Practice for 15 to 30 minutes, or as needed.
