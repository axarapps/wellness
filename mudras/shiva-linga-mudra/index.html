Shiva Linga mudra

Energy-charging

How to

Place your right hand, with thumb extended upward, on top of your left hand, which is shaped like a bowl. Hold the fingers of your left hand close together. Position your hands at the level of the abdomen, elbows pointing outward and slightly forward.

Benefits

  • Helps with tiredness and listlessness
  • Eases dissatisfaction and depression
  • Restores energy after long periods of tension

Practice

Do as often as you like, or two times a day for 4 minutes.
