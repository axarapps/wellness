Merudanda mudra

Central axis

How to

Interlace your fingers and rest your hands in your lap with the thumbs touching and pointing upward, spine held tall.

Benefits

  • Encourages upright posture
  • Strengthens awareness of the spine
  • Steadies the mind for meditation

Practice

Hold while seated with a straight spine for 10 minutes.
