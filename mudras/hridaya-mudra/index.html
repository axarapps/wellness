Hridaya mudra

Heart's ease

How to

Fold your index finger to the base of your thumb, touch the tips of your thumb, middle and ring fingers together, and extend your little finger.

Benefits

  • Eases emotional and physical heart tension
  • Supports circulation
  • Calms anxiety around the chest

Practice

Practice for 5 to 15 minutes with each hand. Avoid straining the fingers.
