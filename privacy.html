Privacy

Last updated: October 4, 2026

What we collect

Nothing. The site has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. We do not receive any data from you.

What stays on your device

If you confirm that you are 18 or older to open the Kama Sutra guide, your browser remembers that on your own device. The browser may also keep copies of the pages so they open faster. Nothing is sent to us.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web server, GitHub may log technical data such as your IP address when you load a page. See the GitHub Privacy Statement. Fonts load from Google Fonts.

Tips

The Support section shows a public Solana address. If you send a tip, the transfer happens in your own wallet and is recorded on the public Solana blockchain. We do not see anything from the website itself.

Health

The guides are general information, not medical advice. Talk to a doctor or qualified teacher if you have a health condition or pain.
